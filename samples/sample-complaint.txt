UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
NORTHERN DISTRICT OF CALIFORNIA

ACME CORPORATION, a Delaware corporation,
                    Plaintiff,
v.
GLOBEX INDUSTRIES LLC,
                    Defendant.

Case No. 24-cv-01287

COMPLAINT FOR BREACH OF CONTRACT

Plaintiff Acme Corporation, by and through its undersigned counsel, alleges as follows:

1. On January 15, 2023, Acme and Globex entered into a Master Services Agreement (the "MSA") for the supply of industrial components.

2. On or about March 10, 2023, Acme delivered the first shipment of components pursuant to the MSA, valued at $1.2 million.

3. From April 1, 2023 to April 30, 2023, Globex's quality assurance team inspected and accepted the delivered components without objection.

4. On June 22, 2023, Globex's Chief Operating Officer, Sarah Chen, sent a written notice of intent to terminate the MSA, citing alleged quality concerns first raised in that same letter.

5. In early September 2023, the parties met in San Francisco in an unsuccessful attempt to resolve the dispute.

6. On November 7, 2023, Globex unilaterally ceased all payments owed to Acme under the MSA.

7. Acme filed this Complaint on February 5, 2024, seeking damages in excess of $4.5 million.

WHEREFORE, Plaintiff demands judgment against Defendant for compensatory damages, pre- and post-judgment interest, costs, and such other relief as the Court deems just.

Respectfully submitted,
Morrison & Hartley LLP
