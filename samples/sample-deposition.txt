DEPOSITION OF SARAH CHEN
Taken on October 14, 2024, at the offices of Morrison & Hartley LLP, San Francisco.

Q. Ms. Chen, please state your full name and current position.
A. Sarah Elizabeth Chen. I am the Chief Operating Officer of Globex Industries LLC.

Q. When did you first become aware of any quality concerns with the components delivered by Acme Corporation?
A. I would say in late May 2023. Our QA team flagged some variance in the tolerance specifications around May 28, 2023, in an internal email.

Q. Did you communicate any of those concerns to Acme before sending the termination notice?
A. There was a phone call on or about June 12, 2023, between myself and Acme's procurement lead, but it was informal. No written notice went out until the June 22 letter.

Q. The complaint alleges that on November 7, 2023, Globex stopped all payments. Is that accurate?
A. The payment stoppage was effective November 1, 2023, not the seventh. November 7 is the date our CFO confirmed the decision in writing.

Q. After the failed September meeting in San Francisco, did the parties have any further direct contact?
A. There was a brief exchange on December 18, 2023, when Acme's CEO called me. The call lasted about ten minutes and produced no resolution.

(Deposition adjourned at 4:45 p.m., to be resumed on October 28, 2024.)
